Burger

Description

A burger is a grilled or fried patty—typically beef—served in a bun and layered with toppings like lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, cheese, and sauces. It’s a simple, flexible classic built around contrast: juicy meat, soft bread, and fresh, tangy, or creamy additions that all come together in a single bite.

Ingredients

  • Burger buns
  • Ground beef (beef mince)
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Cheese slices (e.g., cheddar)
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Tomato (sliced)
  • Onion (sliced)
  • Pickles
  • Ketchup
  • Mustard
  • Mayonnaise
  • Cooking oil or butter (for frying/grilling)

Steps

  • Season the ground beef with salt and black pepper, then form it into even-sized patties.
  • Heat a pan or grill with a little oil or butter.
  • Cook the patties for a few minutes on each side until browned and cooked to your liking. Add cheese on top near the end if using.
  • Lightly toast the burger buns on the pan or grill.
  • Assemble the burger: place lettuce on the bottom bun, then the patty.
  • Add tomato, onion, pickles, and your preferred sauces.
  • Top with the other half of the bun and serve immediately.
