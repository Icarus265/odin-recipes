Pizza

Description

Pizza is a baked flatbread topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and a variety of additional ingredients such as meats, vegetables, and herbs. It’s cooked at high heat until the crust is crisp, the cheese is melted, and the toppings are well combined into a simple, balanced dish.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Dried oregano
  • Fresh basil
  • Garlic (optional)
  • Pepperoni or other meat toppings (optional)
  • Bell peppers (optional)
  • Mushrooms (optional)
  • Onions (optional)

Steps

  • Preheat the oven to a high temperature (around 220°C / 430°F).
  • Roll out the pizza dough into a thin, even base.
  • Spread a layer of tomato sauce over the dough, leaving a small border for the crust.
  • Drizzle a little olive oil over the sauce and season with salt, oregano, and garlic if using.
  • Add a generous layer of mozzarella cheese.
  • Add your chosen toppings (e.g. pepperoni, vegetables, mushrooms, onions).
  • Bake the pizza until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted and bubbling.
  • Remove from the oven, let it cool slightly, then top with fresh basil and slice to serve.
